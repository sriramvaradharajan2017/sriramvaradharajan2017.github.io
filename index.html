1. Privacy Policy

OmniGuard collects no personal data. In-app purchases are processed by Google Play and verified by RevenueCat, which receives your purchase history and an anonymous app identifier for that purpose only. We do not operate user accounts, analytics trackers, advertising SDKs, or cloud sensor-telemetry servers.

1.1 On-Device Sensor Processing & Local Diagnostics

  • No Camera Recording or Upload: Camera frames are analyzed strictly in volatile RAM on your handset to highlight concentrated optical reflections and near-infrared luminance hotspots. No photos or videos are saved, stored, or transmitted.
  • Zero Microphone / Audio Access: OmniGuard explicitly removes and blocks android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO. The application never accesses, records, or listens to your microphone.
  • Zero GPS Coordinate Tracking: OmniGuard never tracks, logs, stores, or transmits your physical GPS coordinates or location history.
  • Local-Only PDF Inspection Logs: Optional Room Privacy Inspection Logs (with a SHA-256 integrity checksum) are generated locally on your device from your active session's optical, wireless, and magnetometer readings and shared only when you explicitly invoke the native OS share sheet.
  • On-Device Diagnostics Log: A diagnostics log of app errors is kept on your device only and is shared solely if you choose to email it to support.

1.2 Device Hardware Permissions & Prominent Disclosure

Why Android & iOS Require Location & Nearby Devices Permissions for Airspace Scanning:

  • Camera & Flashlight (CAMERA / FLASHLIGHT): Used strictly in the foreground while viewing the Optical Glint tab to illuminate and inspect surfaces for lens reflections.
  • Nearby Wi-Fi, Bluetooth LE & Fine Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, NEARBY_WIFI_DEVICES, BLUETOOTH_SCAN): Android and iOS operating systems require Location and Nearby Devices permissions before an app can read nearby Wi-Fi BSSIDs (hardware MAC addresses), hidden SSIDs, and Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) advertisement beacons. OmniGuard uses these signals 100% locally in volatile memory to match hardware IEEE OUI manufacturer prefixes and display signal strength (dBm). Location permissions are never used in the background and GPS coordinates are never read or transmitted.
  • Magnetometer / Compass Sensor: Used strictly in the foreground to measure localized 3-axis electromagnetic field strength (μT) when held within 1 to 2 inches of inspected fixtures.

2. Terms of Service & Advisory Disclaimer

2.1 Permitted Purpose & Classification

OmniGuard is a hardware-assisted room privacy and physical inspection utility intended to augment manual visual checks in hotels, vacation rentals, and travel accommodations using standard consumer smartphone hardware.

2.2 Probabilistic Assessment & Hardware Limitations

OmniGuard relies on consumer smartphone cameras, wireless radios, and magnetometers. It is not an accredited forensic or military counter-surveillance instrument. Detection sensitivity depends on ambient lighting, viewing angle, close physical proximity (1 to 2 inches for EMF), and whether a target device is actively powered or broadcasting. OmniGuard does not guarantee the absence of concealed recording equipment and should always be combined with careful manual physical inspection.

2.3 Responsible Conduct & Limitation of Liability

Sensor readings and risk scores represent statistical anomalies that can also be triggered by legitimate household electronics (such as smart TVs, routers, power adapters, or metallic fixtures). Users agree to verify any anomaly non-destructively and never confront property owners or hospitality staff based solely on automated sensor readings.
